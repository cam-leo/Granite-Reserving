# Case Studies

## When payments speed up

Suppose Granite Lantern Insurance clears a payment backlog in 2024. It pays 900 CAD by 12 months for that accident year, compared with 800 CAD under the original payment schedule. In this scenario, the additional 100 CAD is paid earlier and the ultimate stays at 1,200 CAD.

Apply the historical factor of 1.50 from [Reserving Methods](reserving-methods.md) to both scenarios.

| 2024 scenario | Paid by 12 months (CAD) | Chain ladder ultimate (CAD) | Chain ladder unpaid (CAD) | Scenario unpaid (CAD) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original payment schedule | 800 | 1,200 | 400 | 400 |
| Faster payments | 900 | 1,350 | 450 | 300 |

With faster payments, the historical factor puts the unpaid estimate 150 CAD above the amount implied by the scenario. The calculation interprets the higher early payments as evidence of higher ultimate claims. In a reserve review, check indicators such as claim closure rates and time to payment before deciding whether the historical pattern still applies.
