# Reserving Methods

## Paid chain ladder

Paid chain ladder projects cumulative payments using development patterns observed in older accident years. To estimate a volume-weighted age-to-age factor, include accident years with observations at both ages and divide the sum at the later age by the sum at the earlier age.

Using the [cumulative paid triangle](data-preparation.md), only 2023 has both observations. Its 12-to-24-month factor is $1{,}500 / 1{,}000 = 1.50$. For this example, assume all payments are complete by 24 months.

| Accident year | Paid to date (CAD) | Estimated ultimate (CAD) | Estimated unpaid (CAD) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2023 | 1,500 | 1,500 | 0 |
| 2024 | 800 | 1,200 | 400 |

For 2024, the estimated ultimate is $800 \times 1.50 = 1{,}200$ CAD. The estimated unpaid amount is $1{,}200 - 800 = 400$ CAD.

## Review the assumptions

This factor comes from one accident year and the example assumes full maturity at 24 months. In a reserve review, compare factors across accident years, investigate changes in payment speed and claim mix, and select a tail factor if payments continue past the last observed age. The [payment speed case study](case-studies.md) shows why that review matters.
